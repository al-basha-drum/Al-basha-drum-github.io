About Me

My passion for drumming and Middle Eastern entertainment began at the age of 15, and over the years, I have had the honor of performing alongside numerous celebrities and well-known artists. Through dedication, hard work, and a deep love for my culture, I have built a strong reputation for delivering energetic and unforgettable performances.

As the proud owner of my own professional Dabke and Zaffe team, I specialize in bringing excitement, tradition, and elegance to weddings, engagements, and special events. With years of experience in the entertainment industry, my mission is to create unforgettable moments by combining authentic Middle Eastern traditions with high-energy performances.

For me, drumming is more than just entertainment—it's my passion, my identity, and my way of bringing people together through music and celebration.
